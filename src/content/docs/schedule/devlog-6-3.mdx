1. 1-2 related articles, with summaries ✏️

    [Musical Preferences are Linked to Cognitive Styles](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3138530)

    This study explores how music preferences can correlate with personality traits, thinking styles, and emotional tendencies. It supports the core idea behind my project that entertainment choices are not random, but can reveal patterns about identity and behavior.

    [The Psychology of Media Preferences](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6029792/)

    This article examines how people use movies, television, and music to express themselves socially and emotionally. It connects directly to my project because the experience is built around using media preferences as a form of self-expression and personality analysis.

1. 3 links with summaries of inspiring / related projects ✏️

    [Spotify Wrapped](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-wrapped/)
    
    Spotify Wrapped transforms user listening data into a highly personalized interactive experience. I was inspired by how it makes users feel emotionally connected to their own data and creates highly shareable results.

    [16Personalities](https://www.16personalities.com/)
    
    A modern MBTI-style personality test that presents users with identity “types” and detailed descriptions. I used this as inspiration for how to structure and present personality outcomes in a fun and engaging way.

    [Letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com/)
    
    A social platform centered around movies and taste curation. I was inspired by how people build identity online through their favorite films, rankings, and reviews.

1. Write a summary of your topic(s), research, and technology (3–4 sentences) ✏️

    This project explores how digital entertainment preferences can be transformed into an interactive personality experience. Drawing inspiration from platforms like Spotify Wrapped and personality frameworks such as MBTI, the website analyzes user choices across music, movies, and television to generate a unique personality type. The project combines entertainment APIs, AI-generated interpretation, and interactive web design to create a playful but reflective experience centered around identity and self-expression online. It also investigates why people enjoy seeing themselves represented through personalized data and algorithmic categorization.
